How hours are counted

Each active day closes at midnight. Minutes short of its target are added to what you owe; minutes beyond it pay that back.

What you owe never drops below zero, and extra reading isn't saved for later. Only reading clears it, and nothing resets it.

Days you leave out have no target, but reading on them still pays back what you owe.

How speed is measured

Speed counts only sessions where you noted the page or word you reached. Time without a position still counts toward your target, just not toward speed. Videos and courses, measured in minutes, have no speed.

Last week's speed is everything you read divided by the hours it took, with words counted as pages at {{.WordsPerPage}} a page. It rises when the mix gets lighter, so the bar under it shows what the hours went to.

The index compares each book only with itself. Each week, your speed on every item you also read in the last well-measured week before it is divided by your speed on it then, averaged by time: that is the week's step. The first week is compared with the {{.PaceWindow}} days before the ramp. The index multiplies the steps since the ramp began, so a lighter book, or one printed larger, cannot raise it; if nothing got faster it reads 100%. An item first read in a week counts from the week after.

A session far out of line with its item's own pace (over three times it, or under a third) is left out as a typo.

Each {{.ReviewDay}} the ramp looks at the week just closed. With at least 2 h of such reading and an index at the target, the target rises one step; otherwise it stays. What you owe in hours never holds it.

How the campaign is counted

A book counts when you finish it between the start and the end of the deadline day. Reference, abandoned, and anything that isn't a book never count.

What it needs is the books left, times the average size of the books waiting, divided by your book pace, spread over the weeks left. It moves as you add books and as your pace is measured.

Book pace is every page you noted in a book divided by the hours it took, over the last {{$.PaceWindow}} days. Until 2 h are measured it assumes the books waiting go at the default for their focus ({{$.SeedPaces}} pages/h) and says so.

Only time on books counts here. An hour of articles or video still counts toward your daily target, but it won't finish a book.

Where it's heading uses the book time of your last {{$.ProjectionWeeks}} weeks, leaving out weeks before you started logging.

Your daily target never follows the campaign by itself. Match it when you choose to; nothing already read changes.
