How it works

Each active day closes at midnight. Minutes short of the target are added to what you owe; minutes beyond it pay it down.

What you owe never drops below zero, and extra reading doesn't carry forward.

Only reading clears it. Nothing resets it.

A ramp is checked each {{.ReviewDay}}. It rises one step when nothing is owed and its value has held for a full week; otherwise it holds.

At its ceiling the ramp ends, and the target stays there until you change it.

How speed is measured

Speed counts only sessions where you noted the page or word you reached. Time without a position still counts toward your target, just not toward speed. Videos and courses, measured in minutes, have no speed.

Last week's speed adds up what you read and divides it by the hours it took. Words count as pages at {{.WordsPerPage}} words a page, only so everything fits one number. A week needs 2 hours of such reading before a speed is shown.

That number moves when the mix changes: an hour of light articles reads faster than an hour of a dense book. The line under it shows what the hours went to.

The speed index compares each kind of material only with itself. Its baseline is your speed on it over the {{.PaceWindow}} days before the ramp began. Last week's speed divided by that baseline gives a percentage, and the percentages are averaged by the time each took. If nothing got faster, it reads 100%, whatever you chose to read.

Material first read during a ramp takes that week as its baseline and counts from the week after.

The speed ramp is checked each {{.ReviewDay}}. Its target rises one step when last week's index reached it, the week had 2 hours of measured reading, and the target has held for a full week. Time owed does not hold it.
